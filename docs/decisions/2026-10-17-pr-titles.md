# PR titles: conventional commits

**Status:** accepted, 2026-10-17. Supersedes the plan's "no prefixes" rule for titles.

## The question
A PR's title becomes the squash commit on main, so the titles are the project's history. Should they carry a type and an area, and should that be enforced?

## What we chose
`type(scope)!: Summary`, in the [Conventional Commits](https://www.conventionalcommits.org/) shape, checked on every PR by `.github/workflows/pr-title.yml` (the `pr-title` check). The format, types and scopes are in [the studio's PRs section](../../studio/README.md#prs).

- **Types:** the standard set (`feat`, `fix`, `docs`, `test`, `refactor`, `perf`, `chore`, `ci`), plus two of ours. `spike` keeps throwaway prototypes apart in the history, so release notes can leave them out. `release` is for cutting an alpha or a beta. `build` and `style` are left out: `build` would be confused with the studio's "build" kind of unit, and there's no formatter for `style` to describe.
- **Scope:** optional, and it must be one of the repo's areas. A PR that spans several areas leaves it out rather than picking one.
- **`!`:** breaks something saved, such as levels, replays, saves or scenarios. The sim is deterministic, so a replay that changes its outcome is a real break.
- **Summary:** plain words starting with a capital, no full stop. The producer prefers the capital.

## Also considered
- **Plain titles with no prefix** (the plan's first rule). They read well, but the history can't be filtered or grouped, and release notes would need every title read and sorted by hand.
- **Types named after the studio's unit kinds** (`build`, `shape`, `think`, …). They're closer to the board, but unfamiliar to tools and to anyone outside the studio. The kinds map onto the standard types well enough (see the table in the studio doc).
- **A required scope.** Too many PRs honestly span areas.

## What it means for later
- Release notes (unit 8 of Building the studio) can group by type straight from the history on main.
- The scope list lives in the workflow and grows with the repo. The health check can later check it against the repo map.
- The `pr-title` check should be required in the `main` ruleset, next to `CI`.
