Pixelbob

A 2D platformer where everything, including Bob the blob, is simulated pixels.

Concept

The concept doc: what the game is, how it looks, the worlds, menus and the plan.

Game

The game, from the start. A scenario starts it somewhere, with a card saying what to try:

  • ice - Ice, then the brazier and the spikes (with a handoff card)
  • runny - Runny Bob at the pit (with a handoff card)
  • shell - The game shell (with a handoff card)
  • start - Bob's test course

Studio

The studio: the board (what's in flight, what's waiting on the producer, what's next) and the sessions so far.

Prototypes

  • 00-stills - Art direction stills: one shot per world, Bob drawn from the real goo sim, and a sheet of his states
  • 01-sandbox - World sandbox: dig into rock, pour in sand, water, lava, fire, plants and gases, heat and cool, place lamps
  • 02-bob - Bob playground: roll, jump, freeze, melt and get torn apart on a test course, with live sliders for his physics
