Pixelbob art direction shots

One shot per world (docs/worlds.md) at the 320×180 game camera, and Bob's states. Every screen is drawn procedurally, pixel by pixel, with the lighting model the game uses, and every Bob is the real goo, simulated into his pose. Nothing is a hand-drawn sprite. Use the controls to compare Bob's colours and the light. Tap a shot to enlarge it.

Design notes: docs/ in the repo.
