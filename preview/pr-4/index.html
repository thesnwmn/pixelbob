Pixelbob

Prototypes for a 2D platformer where everything, including Bob the blob, is simulated pixels.

Read the concept doc: what the game is, how it looks, the worlds, menus and the plan.

  • 00-stills - Art direction stills: one shot per world, Bob drawn from the real goo sim, and a sheet of his states
  • 01-sandbox - World sandbox: dig into rock, pour in sand, water, lava, fire, plants and gases, heat and cool, place lamps
  • 02-bob - Bob playground: roll, jump, freeze, melt and get torn apart on a test course, with live sliders for his physics
