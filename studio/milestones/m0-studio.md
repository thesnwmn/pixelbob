## Why
The game is built by a team of agents from here on, one session at a time. They need a loop that holds their state in the repo, keeps main green, and asks the producer only for the producer's calls. See [the studio](../README.md).

## Scope
- The health check (done) and the folder and board.
- The roles and the commands, the docs-in-step checks, and the gate check with the GitHub setup.
- The studio page on Pages.

## Exit criteria
- `/go` on a fresh session picks a unit from the board, ships it in a PR, has the Critic review it, merges it and reports, without help.
- `/next`, `/needs-me`, `/director`, `/feedback` and `/status` do what [the producer guide](../producer-guide.md) says.
- The studio page shows the board on Pages.
- The producer has done the [one-time setup](../producer-guide.md#one-time-setup).
