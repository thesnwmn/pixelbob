## Why
The first slice of the real game (phase 3 of the [roadmap](../../docs/roadmap.md#phase-3-first-level-slice)): does Bob, in levels built around what only this sim can do, make a game that's fun to play?

## Scope
- [The Green](../../docs/worlds.md#1-the-green): three or four levels built from the [puzzle seeds](../../docs/game-overview.md#puzzle-seeds), with checkpoints.
- The level file format, the title screen, the map and saves from the concept doc.
- What the studio needs to test it: the game shell with scenarios and the feedback button, replays, bots, and alpha builds.
- An audio prototype.

## Exit criteria
- An alpha: the milestone's mechanics are all playable end to end; content can be rough.
- Every level is proven finishable by a bot.
- No open `blocker` issues.
- The producer has played the alpha build and rated it.
