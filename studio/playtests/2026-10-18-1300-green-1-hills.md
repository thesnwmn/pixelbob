## What they said
On Android (Chrome 155), 42 s.

"Overall I liked it as an opening level. With added atmosphere (finalized scenery, pan down or something to start, music, sound effects) I think it will land well."

"There is a bug of some form at the start where often I get stuck in the grass or something. It starts rolling and then stops. I have to go back and forward again or tap to jump. Annoying."

"Maybe I'd make an extra jump or two before the first that only require a tap. Teach one jump height. Then a high jump. Then the ravine and a final high jump."

The card's questions:
- Does mashing jump past the steps feel like cheating, or just like play? "No. It's just timing. Different approaches we can play with."
- Is the lost bit at the bottom of the ravine worth climbing back out for? "I like the idea. If we had scores (and badges for completing a level with the maximum amount of bob possible) then it would encourage the player to explore that functionality."

Asked afterwards:
- The tap jumps before the first high step: **now, before ponds** (recommended).
- Scores and badges: **an idea for later** (recommended).

## Wrong
- Stuck at the start: two bugs, both found in the replay.
  - [#35](https://github.com/thesnwmn/pixelbob/issues/35): Bob stalls on gentle grass slopes. From the spawn, holding straight right, he stops on the first rise. Raised during the build; this makes it the first thing a player meets.
  - [#37](https://github.com/thesnwmn/pixelbob/issues/37): on touch, a drag to the right also reads as a little down, which squashes Bob so he barely rolls.

## Liked, and would change
- Liked it as an opening level: fun 4, difficulty right.
- Teach one jump height at a time: a tap jump or two first, then the high step, the ravine and the last high step. [green-1-hills](../work/green-1-hills.md) goes back to ready for it.
- Mashing jump past the steps is fine: "just timing", a different way to play. Leave it.
- Wants atmosphere: finished scenery, a pan down to start, music and sound effects. Decoration isn't a unit yet; the [audio spike](../work/audio-spike.md) is ready.
- Scores and badges for finishing with all of Bob, so the lost bits are worth the detour: in [ideas](../../docs/ideas.md#collectables-and-challenges).
