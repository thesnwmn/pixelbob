## What they said
The search bot on the first Green level, headless in Node (`main` at 5477218 plus [bots](../work/bots.md)). Two routes: the bot's own, and the scripted run in `game/replays/green-1-hills-run.json`, which rolls and jumps from a standstill the way a new player might.

- **Finishable: yes.** `node game/scripts/bot.ts green-1-hills` found a route in 127 s of search (26,690 steps), finishing in **15.5 s** of play with all of Bob and 61% of him home. The scripted run takes 29.6 s; the producer took 42 s. The bot hops nearly all the way, with charged hops over the second bump and the ravine. Kept as `game/replays/green-1-hills-bot.json`.
- **Skips: none.** Both routes touch both checkpoints. Both leave the lost bit in the ravine (it's optional).
- **Softlocks: none found.** From the two low places the search passed through (the ravine floor at x 1044, and the top just past the first checkpoint, at x 749), a short search gets out without going back to a checkpoint: the steps out of the ravine work.

**Hard moments**: jumps Bob can't get on without, and how far each can be off and still work. Up to 20 frames either way and holds up to 40 were tried. Where each is comes from the level's ground: bumps at x 410 and 510, the first high step at 670, the ravine from 1030 to 1070, the second step at 1340.

## Liked, and would change
- The level teaches what it means to, by the numbers. Each bump takes any tap at any time; the first high step takes any timing, but only a hold of 17 frames or more; the second takes 19 or more. A margin of 0 cells means Bob is waiting against the bump or step when he jumps, so the timing doesn't matter.
- The ravine is the one moment with a real window for a player rolling up to it: about 7 frames late, or 24 cells of takeoff. The second step allows only 4 frames early, but plenty late.
- Hopping at speed is much tighter: the bot's charged hop over the second bump and up the high step has a window of 1 frame early and 2 late, 6 cells. That's the skilled way through, and it's twice as fast (15.5 s against 29.6 s), which fits "more ways to play" in the taste notes.
- These are the first numbers for "too precise". The producer rated this level's difficulty "right" ([playtest](2026-10-18-1300-green-1-hills.md)), so they're a baseline: the tightest window on the obvious way through is about 4 to 7 frames, at the ravine and the second step.
