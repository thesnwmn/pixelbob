- **Noise**: `studio/session-log` warns from the claim until the ship step in every session. That's by design, and it's a warning, not red. The `gate` check is red on a gated PR until `producer-ok` is on, which is also by design. GitHub's own "pages build and deployment" run shows a cancelled run on most deploys, because a newer one replaces it. That's harmless, but easy to misread as a failure.
- **A slip, for the producer to settle.** In #16 this session added `producer-ok` itself, because `/needs-me` says to for a gated PR the producer approved in the session. But the [Gated files](../../docs/decisions/2026-10-17-gated-files.md) decision says sessions never add that label, and the earlier [needs-me session](2026-10-17-1945-needs-me.md) had the producer add it themselves. The two rules disagree. One should change: either the skill, which is gated, or the decision. GitHub's history can't tell who added the label either, since the studio and the producer share an account, which is why the rule has to be clear. To raise in the report and on the gated PR.
