## Summary
PR titles now follow `type(scope)!: Summary`, conventional-commits style, and a `pr-title` workflow checks every PR. This replaces the plan's "no prefixes" rule, so it has the first decision record: [PR titles](../../docs/decisions/2026-10-17-pr-titles.md).

## The producer said
- Wants conventional-commit-like PR titles, enforced by an action on the PR, and asked what format suits the studio.
- On the proposal: keep `spike` and `release` as our own types; the scope is optional; the summary starts with a capital, not lowercase. "Do it."

## Done
- `.github/workflows/pr-title.yml`: `amannn/action-semantic-pull-request@v5` on `pull_request_target`. It reads the title only and never checks out code. It checks:
  - the types: `feat fix spike docs test refactor perf chore ci release`,
  - the scopes (optional): the repo's areas,
  - the summary: must not start lowercase, and must not end with a full stop.
- `studio/README.md#prs`: the format, a table from type to the studio's unit kinds, the scopes, what `!` means here (breaks levels, replays, saves or scenarios), and examples.
- `docs/decisions/2026-10-17-pr-titles.md`, the first decision record. It's listed in the docs index, and `docs/decisions/` is no longer "planned" in the repo map.
- The PR template, `CLAUDE.md`, the producer guide (add `pr-title` to the ruleset) and the tech doc's list of workflows are in step.

## Tried and dropped
- **Types named after unit kinds** (`build`, `shape`, `think`). Tools and outsiders don't know them, and the kinds map onto the standard types.
- **A required scope, and a lowercase summary.** The producer chose optional and capitalised.
- **Checking the title in `pnpm health`.** The title lives on GitHub, not in the repo, so a PR check is the right place for it.

## Learned
- The summary pattern `^(?![a-z]).+[^.]$` allows a summary that starts with a `code` name (`` `pnpm health` …``) as well as a capital.
