## Summary
`/go` picked hop-tune: it carries out the producer's answer to hop-speed, and bots waits on it. Chained hops no longer stack speed without limit. A new tweak, `hop`, makes a jump's lean forward fade smoothly past top speed, and makes landings soak up most of the speed past it. On flat ground the best hopping rhythm went from over three times rolling to about 1.6. It's waiting on the producer's playtest, in `?scenario=hops`.

## Tried and dropped
- Cutting the lean off just past top speed: it hit the target (1.5x) but was a switch (a jump at 70.1 px/s against 70.8 went 27 cells further), and the critic caught it.
- Fading the lean from standstill: shortened every jump from a roll.
- Only soaking speed on landing: even soaking all of it left hops at 1.84x. Most of the extra comes from the lean and air steering, not from speed carried through a landing.

## Learned
- With `hop` 0.25, the best of 117 hopping rhythms is 1.67x rolling from the start, the median 1.48x; it was 3.13x and 1.98x.
- Over the hills hopping is still about twice rolling, because rolling uphill is slow.
- Jumps released above top speed (downhill, or a long wind-up, since he speeds up as he squashes) lean less and land a few cells shorter. The gap and the lava are as passable as before (the same number of jump timings work, within one).
