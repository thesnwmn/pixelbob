## Summary
`/go` picked level-format: the milestone's next ready unit, and everything else in The Green builds on it. Levels can now be painted: a folder in `game/levels/` with a material PNG in the level palette and a `level.json`. They load the same way in the browser and in Node, a tool checks them, makes new ones and writes the palette for paint tools, and the health check runs it. A tiny level plays end to end.

## Done
- `PAINT` and `paintedMaterial` in `packages/sim/src/paint.ts`: one colour per material, the middle of its ramp, air black.
- `game/src/png.ts`: a PNG reader with no DOM (indexed at any depth with tRNS, RGB, RGBA, every row filter); Node inflates with zlib, the browser with DecompressionStream.
- `parseLevel` and `addLevel` in `game/src/levels.ts`; `game/scripts/load-levels.ts` for Node, used by the replays, filmstrips and tests; `main.ts` loads the level a scenario needs.
- `game/scripts/levels.ts`: check (also that each level is in `LEVEL_WIDTHS`), `palette <out.gpl>`, `new <id> <w> <h>`. The `code/levels` health check runs it, with broken fixtures (off the map, a missing PNG, a colour not in the palette).
- `game/levels/tiny` and `?scenario=tiny`. In headless Chromium, holding right finishes it in 2.4 s with 97% home and the lost bit picked up.
- Checkpoints moved to ready. Docs: the level format in the tech doc, the repo map, the doc map, CLAUDE.md.

## Learned
- Vite inlines a small PNG as a data URL, and `fetch` reads that like any other URL, so the browser path needs no special case.
- Reading by colour, not index, means a paint tool that reorders the palette is fine, but retuning a ramp's middle colour breaks the levels that use it. `code/levels` says which (noted in paint.ts).
