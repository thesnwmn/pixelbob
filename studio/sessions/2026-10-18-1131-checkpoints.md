## Tried and dropped

## Learned
- "All of Bob lost" can't be `count === 0`: lost bits are Bob particles too, so when his body dies an uncollected bit becomes the main piece. Play now remembers which particles have been in the main piece; a piece he left behind still takes over (the producer likes that), and a bit he never picked up doesn't.
- The Critic noted the replay header stays `v1` though it gained `b`: an older build reading a new replay says "isn't a run". Fine while replays are exact only per build.
