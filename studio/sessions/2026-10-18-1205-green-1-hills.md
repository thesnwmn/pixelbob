The first Green level is painted, with two checkpoints, a lost bit, a scenario with a handoff card and a kept replay that finishes it.

## Tried and dropped
- A 61-cell ravine and steps of 24, 28 and 34: a tap got up the 24, a standing full charge crossed the 61, and the 34 (and 28 to 30 for the second step) failed a good jump too often. Settled on 26 and 56, by measuring each from 8 nearby starts.
- The spike's search bot (with the levels loaded, not committed) stalled at x 906 after the ravine: its score only rewards going right. The kept run is a scripted driver instead: roll right, a full charge (22 frames) at 405, 772 and 1080, and a tap when he hasn't moved for 40 frames.

## Learned
- What Bob can do on grass, for designing levels: he rolls up 0.75 slopes from rest (45° is chancy); a tap jumps about 15 cells high, a full charge about 30 and, from a standstill, across gaps of 80 or more; walls of 26 are sure with a full charge, 30 and up are chancy. Rolling tops out near 85 px/s even downhill, so a run-up barely matters.
- The level was drawn from a height profile (knots joined by straight or eased runs, vertical steps) by a throwaway script, then checked with `node game/scripts/levels.ts`. A level tool that does this could save the Designer painting by hand; not needed yet.
