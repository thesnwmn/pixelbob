## Summary
`/go` picked [#35](https://github.com/thesnwmn/pixelbob/issues/35): nothing needed the producer, no open PRs, and the producer's playtest had made it the first thing a player meets. Bob wasn't stalling on slopes: he was jamming on grass that had grown since the level was painted. A painted level's plants now start dormant.

## Done
- `World.dormant()` (`packages/sim/src/world.ts`): plants and vines lose the growth energy they're placed with, so they only grow where they drink. The painted-level build calls it (`game/src/levels.ts`); the sandbox and the code-built course keep their sprouting.
- Tests: a painted lawn is unchanged after 5 s; Bob rolls more than 100 cells in 3 s from the spawn and from two starts on the first slope (before: 53, 18 and 7); dormant plants still grow when watered.
- `green-1-hills-run.json` re-recorded: the old jump timings assumed the stall. Its driver is now "a full charge when stuck for 20 frames, and at x 772 and 1080"; it finishes in 22.2 s (was 26.1), all of Bob. `green-1-hills-tap.json` only changed its fingerprint.
- A checkpoint test that rolled Bob for a second, assuming he wouldn't reach the tiny level's checkpoint, now rolls half a second and checks nothing is lit.
- Docs: [the level format](../../docs/tech-and-toolchain.md#level-format) and [the simulation](../../docs/simulation.md).

## Tried and dropped
- Suspected first: grass `friction` and the slope's staircase. A fresh world with a plant floor never stalled (0 or 0.4 slopes, many starts), until the world was stepped as well.

## The Tester
448 headless runs across green-1-hills: no stalls left except against walls (the two steps, the ravine's east wall, the world's edge); on open ground he covers 115 to 193 cells in 3 s. On main the same sweep stalled on flat grass and on the 0.47 slope. Grass rolls within about 8% of rock, so no lost grip and no runaway. The tiny level and all 4 replays pass. A full charge gets up both steps every time. But a 4-frame tap gets up the **first** step 5 times in 48 (on main, 5 in 7), and an 8-frame jump always does; the second step needs 12 frames.

## The Critic
Blocking: the PR's description and session log, done at ship. Taken: fewer starts in the slow test, the docs state the rule rather than the story, and `materials.md` says painted vines are dormant too (on purpose: a level is as painted).

## Learned
- Every plant cell is placed with 3 units of growth energy (`put` in `world.ts`), so any painted grass sprouts blades up to 3 cells tall in its first seconds, at seeded random spots. That's why the stall "happened at varied heights" and changed with the world's width: the RNG pattern moved.
- A blade 2 or more cells tall is a wall to Bob's particles (the step-up only takes 1), and rolling from rest he hasn't the speed to ride over it.
- The old scripted run's charge at x 405 never got him up the first step: a tap after it did, by luck.
- The tap replay's `finished: 0` proves little by itself: 600 frames isn't long enough to finish anyway. Its fingerprint is what would catch a change.

## Left for next time
- The first step doesn't quite teach the charged jump: a tap sometimes gets up it (above). [green-1-hills](../work/green-1-hills.md) is back to ready for its tap jumps first, so retune the step then. The second step is the cleaner lesson.
- [#37](https://github.com/thesnwmn/pixelbob/issues/37), the touch drag that reads as down, is the other half of the same playtest complaint.
- Levels with ponds (green-2-ponds) will grow grass next to water, a stubble again. That's the mechanic working, but a designer should know.
