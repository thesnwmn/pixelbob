## Summary
`/go` picked [#37](https://github.com/thesnwmn/pixelbob/issues/37): main was green, nothing needed the producer, there were no open PRs, and the last session named it as next. The touch stick now counts down (and up) by the drag's angle, so a sideways drag that drifts down stays a roll.

## Done
- `game/src/stick.ts`: `readStick(dx, dy)`, moved out of `main.ts` so it runs in Node. Down and up start at 15 px plus 0.75 px per pixel sideways, so only a drag steeper than about 37° below (or above) level counts, and the reading grows smoothly from there, with no switch. Move is unchanged (full at 40 px).
- `game/test/stick.test.ts`: the playtest's drag (full right, 19 to 26 px low) reads no down; long shallow drags read none and long steep ones do; straight down and up are as before; no reading jumps by more than a pixel's worth.
- `stick.ts` is on the no-DOM check's list (`scripts/health/src/code.ts`).
- Docs: the touch controls in [the game overview](../../docs/game-overview.md) and the concept doc; `stick.ts` in [the game's files](../../docs/tech-and-toolchain.md#the-game); `CLAUDE.md`'s list of DOM-free files.

## Tried and dropped
- Capping the sideways part of the dead zone at full move (40 px), as the Tester and the Critic both raised: then a long shallow drag, (100, 60) for example, reads as down again, which is the bug. Instead the dead zone stays purely by angle. A long drag needs a proportionally longer drop to squash, but its angle is the same.

## The Tester
On main, the slowdown in #37's table no longer reproduces. Holding right for 3 s on green-1-hills with down forced to 0, 1/8, 2/8 or 3/8 covers 163, 168, 175 and 185 cells, so a little down is now slightly faster. The table only comes back on 23c88f8, before #40: the crawl was the grass stubble #40 removed, and down only made it worse. So this fix is about input, not speed: a sideways drag no longer squashes him. Across a sweep of drags (dx 20 to 120, dy −40 to 60, with drift and jitter), any drag at full move covers 155 to 214 cells, against 163 for the keyboard. Deliberate squashes and climbs (straight, and steep diagonals) still read full.

## The Critic
Blocking: no test pinned the angle, so a bigger flat dead zone would have passed; that's fixed with the long shallow and long steep cases. Taken: a tighter smoothness bound, and `CLAUDE.md` and the concept doc. Not taken: the cap (above). The Bob playground (`prototypes/02-bob/src/main.ts`) still has the old stick. It's a prototype, so it's left alone.

## Learned
