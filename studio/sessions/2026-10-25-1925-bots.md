A field it can't copy (a Map, a function, another class's object) throws rather than being shared. `Play.copy()` adds the checkpoints and the replay so far. ## Tried and dropped
- Judging a jump against where the route was a fixed time later. That flags any jump that only makes Bob faster (on `tiny`, a charge on flat ground). Now a jump is hard only if, without it, he never gets to where the route was before its next jump. He gets extra time to roll there, and later jumps can't rescue him.
- Requiring him to be no lower than the route ever went. That flagged a hop over a dip, because rolling through the dip is lower. Now he only has to be above the ground the route was over.

## Learned
- About 210 steps a second on green-1-hills (1631 × 200 cells), copies included. A search takes about 2 minutes; measuring a route's slack takes about 4.
- The slack separates the level's teaching moments cleanly. Bumps and steps have no timing window, because Bob waits against them; what matters is how long he holds (17 frames or more for the first step). The ravine is the one real window, about 7 frames.

## Left for next time
- The score is "further right". A level with a detour or backtracking (ponds, moss) will need waypoints in `level.json`, or distance along a route.
- Levels have no names for their places (`note(x)` is empty for painted levels), so the report names hard moments from the ground profile by hand.
- Scoring Bob's fun is still the producer's job: the numbers are a baseline against the "right" difficulty rating green-1-hills got.
